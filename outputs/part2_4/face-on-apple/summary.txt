Part 2.4 multiresolution blend: face-on-apple
Description: The portrait's eyes align across the apple; three overlapping ellipses make an irregular face-shaped mask that selects the portrait.
Pyramid levels (largest to smallest): 512x512, 256x256, 128x128, 64x64, 32x32, 16x16
Blend formula at each level: M * L1 + (1 - M) * L2
The input mask is binary and unchanged. Its Gaussian pyramid softens the transition over wider areas at coarser levels. Source Laplacian pyramids and the blended reconstruction are stored as floating-point .npy files.
