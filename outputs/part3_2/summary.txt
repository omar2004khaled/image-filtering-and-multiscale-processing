Part 3.2: Results and Comparisons
Each working image is capped at 1000 pixels on its longest side before comparison, following the assignment's medium-size image guidance.
The target width is the working width minus round(0.30 * working width); the PDF says about 30% and gives no further rounding rule. All three narrowed methods preserve height and use that same target width.
Normal resizing uses OpenCV INTER_AREA. Center cropping removes equal horizontal margins. Seam carving uses Part 1.2 finite-difference magnitude for the six standard comparisons.
